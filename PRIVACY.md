# Privacy policy — Rezina Loop Machine

Rezina Loop Machine does not collect, store remotely or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission and never connects to the network.
- It asks for no permissions. It reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) only to shape the sound, on the device, while the app is open.
- Loops you save stay in the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- There are no ads, analytics or third-party libraries.
